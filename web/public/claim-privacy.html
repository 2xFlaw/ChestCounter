Pirate Shop Claim All privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy covers the Chest Counter: Pirate Shop Claim All browser extension (Chrome Web Store and other browsers) and the matching userscript, published by 2xFlaw. For questions, contact flxwlesslybusiness@gmail.com. The extension is an independent project and is not made, endorsed or supported by Epic Games.

1. Summary

The extension does not collect, sell or share your personal information. It has no analytics, no advertising and no account of its own. Nothing it handles is sent to Chest Counter or to 2xFlaw.

2. What it does on fortnite.com

The extension only runs on https://www.fortnite.com/*. When you choose to claim, it reads the Item Shop page you are viewing and sends the claim requests to Epic Games from your own browser, using the fortnite.com session you are already signed in with. Those requests go directly to Epic, as if you had pressed each claim button yourself, and are covered by Epic Games' Privacy Policy. The extension never reads or stores your Epic password, and it does not copy your session or security tokens anywhere outside the fortnite.com page.

3. What is stored on your device

The extension uses the browser's local extension storage for:

  • your settings (the optional daily reminder and the option to skip the review screen);
  • simple totals such as how many items you have claimed and when;
  • whether the Pirate Shop looked active on your last visit and when it ends, so the toolbar badge and reminder can work.

A copy of the totals (number of runs, items and cosmetics claimed, their approximate V-Bucks value and the time of the last run) is also kept in the fortnite.com site storage, under ccClaim.stats, so the page can show it. All of this information stays in your browser. Removing the extension deletes its own storage; clearing site data for fortnite.com removes the page copy. The "Save list" button creates a text file of claimed items on your own computer only when you press it.

4. Other network requests

The popup checks a small public file on GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com/2xFlaw/ChestCounter/.../version.json) to tell you when a newer version exists. That request contains no personal information, though, like any web request, GitHub receives your IP address and browser details; see GitHub's Privacy Statement. Links in the popup open chestcounter.io only when you click them, and that site is covered by the main Chest Counter privacy policy.

5. Permissions

  • storage: saves the settings and totals described above.
  • alarms and notifications: show the optional daily reminder at 00:02 UTC while your Pirate Shop is active. No notification is shown unless you turn the reminder on in the popup.
  • Access to www.fortnite.com: needed to read the Item Shop and send claim requests on that site. The extension cannot read any other website.

6. Chrome Web Store Limited Use

The use of information received from Google APIs and browser extension permissions adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. No user data is transferred to third parties, used for advertising or creditworthiness, or read by humans.

7. Children

The extension does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. Use of fortnite.com is subject to Epic Games' own age rules and parental controls.

8. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated. Questions or requests: flxwlesslybusiness@gmail.com. Source code: github.com/2xFlaw/ChestCounter.
