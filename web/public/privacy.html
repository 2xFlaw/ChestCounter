Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This notice explains how Chest Counter, operated under the name 2xFlaw, handles information when you use chestcounter.io and the optional online features of the Windows app. For privacy questions or requests, contact flxwlesslybusiness@gmail.com. Chest Counter is an independent project, not an Epic Games service.

1. Local app use and optional online sync

The app processes Fortnite replay information on your computer. Online match uploads require linking the app to a website profile and enabling sync. Full personal-history sync, including manual additions and imported replay statistics, has an additional approval step in the app. Disabling uploads stops future sync but does not automatically erase information already uploaded.

The sync features described here upload statistics, not replay files, Epic login credentials, player names, teammate names or the text of manual notes. A public username is sent if you choose to set one. Do not send us passwords or device tokens by email.

2. Information processed

  • Website profile and access: a generated profile ID, an optional public username, privacy settings, a starting-total setting, linked-PC record IDs, linking times, last-seen times, and authentication records.
  • Synced match statistics: match/session identifiers, start times, game modes, opened and destroyed chest counts, chest-type counts, chest-opening times, match length, and the total number of chests your teammates opened (a count only, never their names).
  • Optional personal-history snapshots: replay and imported-match statistics, dated manual chest amounts and entry IDs, optional teammate count contributions according to your app settings, a generated source identifier, and a hashed app-profile scope. These snapshots are separate from competitive ranking records.
  • Social and streaming features: friend-group names and membership, and private OBS-link credentials if you choose to create them.
  • Treasure Hunt ticks: the weekly rewards you mark as found and when each mark was last changed, saved to your profile so the website and a linked app show the same ticks. A copy is also kept in your browser's local storage (cc.hunt) and, on a linked PC, in the app's data folder.
  • Technical information: requests necessarily disclose information such as IP address, browser information and requested URLs to the services delivering the website. Hosting and advertising providers may process additional technical information under their own policies.
  • Anonymous player rankings: to show how your dashboard figures compare with other players, the app sends a random install ID and summary figures (number of matches, chest totals, averages, best match and chests per minute). It does not send names, Epic IDs, match details or replay files, and you do not need to link a PC. If your PC is linked, its link token is included so you are not counted twice. You can turn rankings off on the app's dashboard, and figures that have not been updated for 180 days are no longer used.

3. How information is used

Information is used to authenticate your browser and linked app, provide your statistics and history, calculate reward-chance estimates, maintain optional social and streaming features, display eligible public rankings, handle support requests, and operate and secure the service. Manual additions and editable starting totals are excluded from the public leaderboard calculations. Advertising, where enabled, helps fund the service.

Where applicable data-protection law requires a legal basis, essential service processing is based on providing features you request and legitimate interests in operating and securing the service, as appropriate. Optional advertising storage and personalised advertising rely on consent where required. Statutory obligations may require some records to be retained.

4. Public information and sharing

A username and eligible match-derived statistics can appear on public leaderboards when you have a linked PC and private mode is off. Profile-based luck information may be accessible using a profile ID when the profile is not private. Friend-group members can see statistics made available through the group feature. Anyone holding a private OBS link may be able to view the statistics it exposes; keep that link private and revoke it when no longer needed.

Private mode restricts public features supported by that setting; it does not remove data already seen or copied by others. We use service providers to deliver hosting, advertising and related functions, as described below.

5. Cookies and browser storage

  • cc_profile: an essential profile/authentication cookie, set with a maximum lifetime of one year.
  • cc_session: an essential browser sign-in cookie used by the app-to-website sign-in flow, set with a maximum lifetime of 30 days.
  • Language preferences: the cc_lang cookie remembers a language choice for up to one year. The cc.lang local-storage entry remains until changed or cleared. The language in a supported URL takes priority; without a saved choice, the main pages may use the country supplied by Cloudflare to choose a default, with browser language or English as a fallback. You can change the language with the picker or clear these preferences through browser settings.
  • Local storage: preferences such as the motion toggle and luck-planner selections remain in your browser until changed or cleared.
  • Google services: where advertising or consent messaging is enabled, Google and participating partners may use cookies, local storage, identifiers or similar technologies for consent management, ad delivery, measurement, frequency control, fraud prevention and, where permitted, personalisation. The applicable consent message provides details of purposes and partners.

Browser settings can block or clear cookies and local storage. Clearing essential profile cookies can cause you to lose browser access to that profile until you sign in again through a linked app. Rejecting optional advertising consent does not itself unlink your PC or delete your statistics.

6. Google advertising, consent messages and fonts

Chest Counter is setting up Google AdSense and Google's Privacy & messaging services. Their availability depends on site approval, deployment, region and account configuration. This notice does not mean an advertising or consent feature is already active on every page.

Where enabled, third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's previous visits to this or other websites. Google's advertising cookies enable Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You can manage personalised advertising through Google My Ad Center and find information about other vendors' choices at aboutads.info/choices. These controls are separate from this site's consent choices.

Read how Google uses information from partner sites and Google's Privacy Policy. Some website pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which involves browser requests to Google. This privacy page uses system fonts and does not load advertising scripts.

If an ad-block recovery message is enabled, it may ask you to allow ads. Dismissing that message does not constitute consent to optional advertising cookies.

7. Hosting and international processing

The website and its database are hosted using Cloudflare. Cloudflare processes information needed to deliver and protect the service; see Cloudflare's Privacy Policy. Downloads and source code are hosted on GitHub; see GitHub's Privacy Statement. Following a download or external-service link sends requests to that provider.

These providers operate internationally, so information may be processed outside your country. Provider policies explain their processing locations and relevant transfer safeguards.

8. Retention and deletion

Match and personal-history records do not currently have an automatic age-based deletion schedule. They are retained to provide history until you delete them through available controls, request deletion, or the operator removes information that is no longer needed, subject to legal and security requirements. Cookie and session expiry does not itself delete match history. Hosting or backup copies may not disappear immediately; contact us with questions about a deletion request.

Removing or unlinking a PC revokes that linked record when the server request succeeds; it does not erase previously uploaded matches or an existing personal snapshot. Stopping personal-history sync also leaves the most recent snapshot on the website. To clear a personal snapshot, stop its uploads in the source app and then use the separate website control. The My stats page also provides Delete my data for your profile. Local files on your computer are managed separately.

9. Your choices and rights

Use the app to enable or stop uploads, and My stats to manage linked PCs, your username, supported privacy settings and deletion controls. When Google's consent message is available, use Consent, Do not consent, or Manage options to make your advertising choices. Use the site's Privacy and cookie settings or consent-revocation link, when displayed, to revisit them. If those controls are missing or not working, contact us; this page does not substitute for a functioning consent platform.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or objection, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data-protection authority. Email the contact above. We may need information sufficient to verify control of the relevant profile, but will not ask you to email your password or device token.

10. Children and young users

If you are a parent or guardian with concerns about a child's use or information, contact us. Advertising features must be configured to meet applicable child-protection requirements. Do not provide unnecessary personal information in a public username or friend-group name.

11. Changes and contact

We may update this notice as features and providers change. The date above indicates the latest revision. For questions about processing, retention, providers or your rights, contact flxwlesslybusiness@gmail.com.
