Activate it in game first

Owning the token isn't enough. Activate the Pirate Shop in the in-game Fortnite Item Shop, then use Claim All on fortnite.com.

Epic's rules for the Pirate Shop:

  • Items sold for V-Bucks are free to claim for 14 days from the moment you activate it.
  • Real-money offers, gifting and pass levels or rewards are excluded.
  • Every sidekick is free once. You can buy extra copies of a sidekick, but Claim All only takes one of each.
  • You can activate it once in 2026 and once in 2027. It isn't transferable.

How it works

About a minute for a full shop.

Activate

Activate the Pirate Shop in the in-game Item Shop.

Sign in

Sign in at fortnite.com/item-shop with the same account.

Choose

Press Claim all free next to Buy V-Bucks, then pick your categories.

Done

Every free item lands in your locker and your V-Bucks stay untouched.

Install

Use one or the other, not both.

Chrome, Edge, Brave and Opera

Browser extension
  1. Download the zip and unzip it. Keep the folder somewhere you won't delete it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder.
  4. Pin the pirate chest to your toolbar to see how many free items are waiting.
Download for Chrome

Firefox, Safari and other browsers

Userscript
  1. Install a userscript manager: Violentmonkey or Tampermonkey on Firefox, or Userscripts on Safari.
  2. Open the userscript and press Install when your manager asks.
  3. It updates itself automatically when a new version comes out.

What it does

Review before claiming

See how many items there are and what they're worth, then choose the categories you want.

One of each sidekick

Every free sidekick is claimed once. Ones you already have are skipped, so you never get extra copies.

Safety stop

Every claim is sent with a price of 0, and it stops straight away if your V-Bucks balance ever drops.

Handles slowdowns

If Epic asks it to slow down, it backs off and carries on instead of failing.

Retry in one click

Anything that failed or was stopped can be claimed again with one button.

Badge and reminders

The Chrome extension shows how many free items are waiting and can remind you when the shop refreshes.

Save your list

Download a list of everything you claimed.

FAQ

Is this a mod or a cheat?

No. It only runs on fortnite.com in your browser and never touches the game or your PC. It does the same thing as pressing Claim on every item yourself, just faster.

Can it spend my V-Bucks?

No. Every claim is sent with an expected price of 0, so Epic rejects anything that isn't free. Your balance is also checked before, during and after each run.

It says the Pirate Shop isn't active.

Activate it in the in-game Item Shop first. Activating starts the 14-day clock.

Can I claim every sidekick?

Yes. Each sidekick is free once with the Pirate Shop. Claim All claims one of each and skips any you already have.

The shop changed. Can I claim again?

Yes. The shop refreshes every day at 00:00 UTC, so you can come back for new items for the whole 14 days.

Does it claim every Jam Track?

Yes. Since v2.5.1 it also reads the full list from the Jam Tracks View all page, not just the tracks shown on the main shop page.

Does it work on mobile or console?

The Chrome extension needs a desktop browser. The userscript also works in Safari on iPhone and iPad. Items you claim show up everywhere you play.

Does it see my password?

No. It runs only on fortnite.com, uses the session you're already signed in with and sends nothing anywhere else. The code is open source, so anyone can check it.

How do I update it?

The extension popup tells you when a new version is out. Unzip the new version over the old folder, then press reload on the extension card. The userscript updates itself.

Why isn't it on the Chrome Web Store?

Loading it unpacked lets you read every line of code you run. A store listing may follow.
